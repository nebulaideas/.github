## Who we are

Nebula Ideas is a software engineering consultancy. We help organizations understand their current reality, identify friction, and uncover practical opportunities for AI, automation, and operational improvement **before** investing in a solution.

Our work starts with shared understanding, not a proposal.

## The Nebula Clarity Sprint

A focused engagement that maps your reality before anyone writes code.

**What we seek to understand**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Context & Objectives** | What the organization is trying to achieve and what matters most |
| **Ways of Working** | How work actually flows across teams, processes, and systems |
| **Technology & Constraints** | What capabilities exist today and what may be limiting progress |
| **AI & Automation Opportunities** | Where AI can create meaningful value and support better decision-making |

**What you get**

- A clear view of the current situation
- Key risks and points of friction
- Prioritized opportunities
- Practical recommendations for next steps
- Guidance on AI and automation
- An initial action plan

Typically completed within 1–2 weeks, depending on organizational complexity and scope. Bilingual delivery in English and Spanish, working with organizations in Mexico and the United States.

- **Rust** — CLI tooling, MCP servers, and performance-sensitive components
